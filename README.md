# IT3310-ProjectPortfolio

Welcome to my professional project portfolio for IT 3310: IT Project Management at Governors State University.

Hello! My name is Arianna McGhee. As an Associate Consultant at NovaTech Consulting, I am documenting my semester-long work and artifact development for our client engagement with Riverside Community Health Network (RCHN) a nonprofit health network operating six community clinics across the Chicago metropolitan area. RCHN is modernizing its aging patient intake and scheduling system, moving from legacy problems to a streamlined, secure, and user-friendly digital solution that serves both clinic staff and patients efficiently.

This repository serves as a living professional record of my project management decisions, analyses, and deliverables as we navigate through initiation, planning, execution, monitoring, and closure of this project.
