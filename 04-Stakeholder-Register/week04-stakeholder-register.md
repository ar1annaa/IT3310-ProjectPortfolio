## Engagement Strategy Reflection

### Highest Priority Stakeholder
The stakeholder in this register that requires the most deliberate engagement is the RCHN Chief Medical Officer. This is because the CMO balances high operational interest with serious decision-making influence, directly connecting executive leadership to clinical reality across all the six clinic locations. If we overlooked the CMO's concerns or information needs, the project could suffer serious clinical disruption and stakeholder pushback and irritation. Without active engagement to ensure data accuracy and clinical workflow safety, the new intake and scheduling system might introduce friction that compromises patient care quality, leading clinicians to resist or reject the platform entirely post-launch.

### Risk of Omission
I initially thought the clinic intake coordinators should be classified under "Manage Closely" because I was unsure whether their day-to-day operational role translated to high formal decision-making influence. However, new information that would cause me to formally reclassify and add them to this level of engagement would be observing that their daily user adoption is actively stalling the workflow rollout, or receiving direct feedback that their informal pushback is creating critical problems across the six clinics.
