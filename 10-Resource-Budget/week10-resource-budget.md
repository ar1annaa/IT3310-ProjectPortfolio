## Estimation Approach

I used bottom-up estimation combined with course parametric rates. Because our project schedule doesn't define exact hours or compensation per role, I chose realistic industry rates and estimated weekly hours that match the effort needed across our 16-week timeline. The limitation of this method is that it relies on my own estimates of how long the work will take, which could change if technical issues come up.

## Contingency Reserve

### Risk-Based Cost Exposure
- Risk 1 (Unresolved migration data discrepancies) — Potential Cost Exposure: $18,000
- Risk 3 (Local clinic internet infrastructure gaps) — Potential Cost Exposure: $12,000

**Recommended Contingency Reserve:** $30,000

Instead of using a percentage, this reserve directly combines our calculated risk exposures ($18,000 for Risk 1 + $12,000 for Risk 3). This ensures we have dedicated funds ready to fix critical data integrity problems and clinic connectivity issues without blowing the project budget.

## Budget Tradeoff Analysis

Before agreeing, I would analyze the impact of a 10% budget reduction on our milestones, staffing allocations, and risk mitigation strategies to ensure we do not compromise system stability, data integrity, or staff training readiness. 

* **Tradeoff 1:** Reducing labor hours or development rates could force us to cut back on data scrubbing and reconciliation passes (mitigation for Risk 1), which directly increases the danger of unresolved migration data discrepancies causing scheduling conflicts at launch.
* **Tradeoff 2:** Cutting non-labor or training funds could eliminate backup hotspots or after-hours training sessions for the two lagging clinics (mitigation for Risks 2 and 3), leading to severe check-in problems and adoption failures during peak morning hours.
